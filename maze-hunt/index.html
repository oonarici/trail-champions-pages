Maze Hunt

Change the paths. Change the chase.

An offline, single-player, turn-based maze puzzle. Race a rival bot while the Warden drone moves twice each turn. WALL seals a passage, TURN reverses conveyors, and a clever route can lure the Warden onto your rival.

Explore 60 hand-checked mazes in six station chapters. Play the first 10 free. One non-consumable Full Game purchase at US$3.99 unlocks mazes 11–60. The local price is shown in the store. Hints and undo are always free.

No ads. No tracking. No account. No data collected by the game. Gameplay works offline; buying or restoring Full Game needs an internet connection.
