Frequently asked questions

What is free, and what does Full Game unlock?

The first 10 mazes are free. One non-consumable Full Game purchase at US$3.99 unlocks mazes 11–60, giving you all 60 mazes. The local price is shown in the store. Hints and undo are always free.

How do I reach the Full Game screen?

Clear maze 10, then continue to maze 11 to see the Full Game screen.

How do I restore Full Game?

Connect to the internet, use the Apple Account that bought Full Game, and choose Restore Purchases on the Full Game screen. If restoring fails, please email us with the message shown in the game.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Gameplay works offline. Buying or restoring Full Game needs an internet connection. No game account is required. There are no ads or tracking, and the game collects no data.

Can you restore lost progress?

Progress is saved only on your device. The game has no account or online sync. If the local data is gone, we cannot recover it for you.

Why can't I hear sound?

Check your device's volume and any sound control in the game. If sound still does not work, please email us.

What happens if I reinstall the game?

Deleting the game removes its locally saved progress and settings. A new install starts without that data.
